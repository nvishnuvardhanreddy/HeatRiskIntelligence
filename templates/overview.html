THE PROBLEM

Heat is more than temperature.

Extreme heat affects people differently depending on humidity, wind, solar radiation, exposure and vulnerability. A single temperature value cannot communicate the complete human risk.

WHY IT MATTERS

Act before conditions peak.

Clear thermal-stress information can support public awareness, outdoor-work planning, health guidance and earlier local action.

WHO BENEFITS

People and decision-makers.

Public users, youth and students, outdoor workers, vulnerable populations, researchers, government and disaster-management teams.

HTSI · HUMAN THERMAL STRESS INDEX

A common language for risk

HTSI combines calculated thermal metrics and environmental stress components, then normalizes the result from 0 to 100 for clear interpretation.

0–20 LOW21–40 MODERATE41–60 HIGH61–80 VERY HIGH81–100 EXTREME

ALERTS & HEALTH

Contextual warnings, not false certainty

Heat alerts are based on actual calculated or modelled risk thresholds. The platform distinguishes calculated thermal risk from an ML health-risk estimate and does not claim clinical accuracy.

View heat-risk details

CURRENT SCOPE

Implemented now, extended later

Currently implemented: public dashboard, real weather integration, calculations, forecast, search, GPS, map interaction, what-if simulation, health-risk seam and deployment configuration.

Limitations: UTCI/WBGT are screening approximations, ward boundaries depend on available data, and no health-outcome dataset is bundled. Future scope should be added only with validated datasets and authoritative GIS sources.
